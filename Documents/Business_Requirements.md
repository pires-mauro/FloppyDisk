

# Problem statement revised

**Who is affected:** Potential new clients, the onboarding officer and governance officer are affected.

**What they cannot do today:** Staff evaluates clients manually against constantly changing regulations. As this is a very subjective process, 2 different onboarding officers can produce 2 different results for the same client. This inconsistency can lead to clients receiving misleading and wrong risk scores. If unchecked, the bank could allow a high-risk person to become a client without even noticing it, because of a mistake by an onboarding officer. Additionally, it is more difficult for governance officers to verify how decisions were made and keep track of what decisions with a client have been made.

**What it costs:** Due to this tedious process, a lot of time can be wasted, which could result in loosing potential clients, as the process takes too long. Furthermore, potential penalties and other consequences can be applied if illicit a person is allowed to open an account.
