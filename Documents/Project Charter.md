## 01 Project Purpose & Business Justification

### Business Problem

The bank currently relies heavily on manual evaluation of new clients. This process requires significant employee time and effort, particularly because internal policies and international regulations change regularly.

### Objective

Develop a standardised digital onboarding process with automated client risk evaluation.

## 03 Project Scope

| Scope | Description |
|---|---|
| **In Scope** | Onboarding interface for onboarding officers; Collection and storage of required client information; Automated weighted risk scoring; Risk classifications such as Low, Medium, High Risk; Red-flag detection; Interface for authorised users to update risk weights; Onboarding experience for private individuals; Traceability of risk scoring |
| **Out of Scope** | Verification of client information; Customer self-service onboarding; Advanced management analytics; Payment or transaction processing; Ongoing transaction monitoring after onboarding; Onboarding experience for companies; GDPR compliance; Dealing with multiple personal ID cases; Changing client data after onboarding |
