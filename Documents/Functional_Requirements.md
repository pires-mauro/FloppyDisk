| **FR-07** | Scoring Engine                    | REQ-03          | **Weighted Risk Scoring Engine:**The scoring engine computes a composite score using the weighted formula:$\text{Total Score} = \frac{\sum (w_i \times v_i)}{\sum w_i}$where**$w_i$**is the configured field category weight (summing to 100%) and**$v_i \in [1, 5]$**is the risk score of the selected parameter value. Final scores map to:• Low Risk:**$1.00 \le \text{Score} < 2.50$**• Medium Risk:**$2.50 \le \text{Score} < 3.75$**• High Risk:**$3.75 \le \text{Score} \le 5.00$** | • Given a pre-configured reference test profile, engine output matches the calculated weighted average to 2 decimal places.• Given a calculated score of 2.49, system classifies tier as 'Low Risk'.• Given a calculated score of 2.50, system classifies tier as 'Medium Risk'.       | Must-Have          |
